Screen Capture

Grab exactly what you need, the way you need it.

  • Full screenOne keystroke captures the whole display.
  • Active windowSnap the frontmost window, with or without its shadow.
  • Window controlPick a single control or panel inside a window.
  • Scrolling windowStitch long pages and documents into one tall image.
  • Fixed regionReuse a saved size and position for consistent shots.
  • RegionDrag a rectangle with pixel-accurate guides.
  • FreehandDraw any shape; everything outside is transparent.
  • Repeat lastRe-take the previous capture area instantly.
  • DelaySet a countdown to catch menus and hover states.
  • Cursor optionInclude, hide, or highlight the pointer.
  • Multi-monitorCapture any display or span them all.
  • Auto-saveEvery shot lands in your chosen folder.
  • Custom file namesTemplates like {app}-{date}-{counter}.

Screen Recording

Short clips and walkthroughs, no extra software.

  • Full screen, window, or regionRecord whatever you point at.
  • System audio + micMix app sound and your voice, with separate levels.
  • FPS and qualityChoose 15 to 60 fps and a quality preset.
  • MP4 outputH.264 files that play everywhere.

Image Editor

A ribbon-style editor that opens the moment you capture.

  • TextFonts, outlines and background fills.
  • Arrows and linesStraight, curved, dashed, with custom heads.
  • ShapesRectangles, ellipses, polygons and rounded boxes.
  • CalloutsSpeech bubbles that point at the detail.
  • Numbered stampsAuto-incrementing steps for tutorials.
  • Pencil, brush, marker, airbrush, calligraphyFive pen styles with pressure support.
  • Crop and resizeTrim, scale, and set canvas size.
  • FillFlood fill with tolerance control.
  • EraserClean up strokes or erase to transparency.
  • RetouchSpot-heal, smudge and clone small blemishes.

Effects & Filters

Polish or protect an image in a click.

  • Blur, sharpen, mosaicHide private details or crisp up text.
  • Grayscale and invertQuick tonal changes.
  • FramesBorders, torn edges and device bezels.
  • ShadowsSoft drop shadows for floating look.
  • WatermarkText or logo, with opacity and position.
  • Brightness and contrastSliders with live preview.
  • Hue and saturationShift or mute colour.
  • Color balanceTune shadows, mids and highlights.
  • Rotate and flip90-degree turns, free angle, mirror.

Design Tools

Little instruments for people who care about pixels.

  • PaletteSave and reuse swatches across sessions.
  • Pixel rulerMeasure on screen in points or pixels.
  • MagnifierZoom up to 32x under the cursor.
  • CrosshairFull-screen guide lines for alignment.
  • ProtractorMeasure angles between any two lines.
  • WhiteboardSketch on a blank or screen-frozen canvas.

FAQ

Which macOS versions are supported?

macOS 13 Ventura and later, on Apple silicon and Intel.

Does SnapDesk need Screen Recording permission?

Yes. macOS asks once; grant it in System Settings, Privacy & Security.

Does it send any data?

No. There are no network calls for analytics, updates of your images, or accounts.

Can I change the shortcuts?

Not yet; the defaults are fixed in this version.

Where are files saved?

In the folder you choose, named by your file-name template.
